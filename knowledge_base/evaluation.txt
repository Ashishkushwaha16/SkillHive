Intern Performance Evaluation
Interns are assessed on the following criteria:
1. Quality (accuracy of work)
2. Timeliness (meeting deadlines)
3. Technical and Soft Skills
4. Initiative and proactivity
Development: Interns are encouraged to use platform resources for skill enhancement and actively engage with mentors for career guidance.
Outcomes: Exceptional performance may be recognized with a Certificate of Excellence or a Letter of Recommendation.
