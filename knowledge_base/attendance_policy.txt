Attendance Policy
Daily log-in is mandatory for all interns. Interns must log in at the start of each workday on the SkillHive platform or the designated communication channel.
Working Hours: Interns are required to follow the standard working hours according to their assigned role and time zone.
Planned Leave: For exams or personal commitments, interns must inform their mentor or HR at least one week in advance.
Sick or Emergency Leave: In case of illness or emergencies, interns should immediately notify their mentor and HR.
